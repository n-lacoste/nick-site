Album Score Rationale

Albums are scored out of 100%, with the following weights:

  • 45% = % of songs "liked" (scored 7+)
  • 35% = Average song score normalized to /100
  • 20% = % of songs "saved" (scored 5+)

Example: Album A has 5/10 liked, 10/10 saved, and an average song score of 6.5/10.

0.45 × (5/10) + 0.20 × (10/10) + 0.35 × (6.5/10)
= 0.225 + 0.2 + 0.2275
= 0.6525
Score: 65.25%
